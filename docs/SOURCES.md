# Authoritative Clinical Sources & Provenance Registry

All clinical rules and phlebotomy instructions are sourced exclusively from current international health guidelines and laboratory standards.

---

## Registered Authoritative Sources

### 1. WHO Guidelines on Drawing Blood: Best Practices in Phlebotomy
* **Source ID:** `SRC_WHO_01`
* **Organization:** World Health Organization (WHO)
* **Publication Date:** 2010 / Current Standards
* **URL:** [WHO Phlebotomy Guidelines](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241599221)
* **Scope:** Universal infection prevention, patient identification, antiseptic application, skin drying times, sharps disposal, and post-procedure care.

### 2. CLSI GP41-Ed7: Collection of Diagnostic Venous Blood Specimens
* **Source ID:** `SRC_CLSI_01`
* **Organization:** Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute (CLSI)
* **Edition:** 7th Edition
* **URL:** [CLSI GP41 Standard](https://clsi.org/standards/products/methodology/documents/gp41/)
* **Scope:** Standard order of draw, 15–30° needle entry angle, 1-minute tourniquet limit, vein selection hierarchy (median cubital $\to$ cephalic $\to$ basilic), tube inversions, and hemolysis prevention.

### 3. WHO Guidelines on Hand Hygiene in Health Care
* **Source ID:** `SRC_WHO_IPC`
* **Organization:** World Health Organization (WHO)
* **URL:** [WHO Hand Hygiene Guidelines](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241597906)
* **Scope:** Mandatory hand sanitation before and after invasive clinical procedures.
